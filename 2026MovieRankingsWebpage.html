A brief summary and ranking of the movies I watched in theaters in 2026.

6. Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother

  • Summary: A religiously devout father and hitman tries to find meaning for both himself and his family in the wake of his wife's death. Layered with commentary, some of it landed for me, most of it didn't. I struggled to understand who this movie was for. Maybe my IQ was too low. I really appreciated its potential though.
  • Rating: 1.9/5

5. Resident Evil

  • Summary: Set in the iconic video game world, Resident Evil follows a wimpy protagonist as he journeys into Raccoon City over the course of one chaotic night. I thought it was pretty fun, and the first act especially built tension well but it relied on a lot of jumpscares. So it wasn't genuinely good horror as much as it was entertaining cheap thrills. Nothing wrong with that, I still enjoyed. My experience was also compromised because these guys behind me talked full volume the entire time. The worst.
  • Rating: 3.2/5

4. Project Hail Mary

  • Summary: Saw this a long time ago but I thought it was a cool, heartwarming traveler sci-fi. The stakes were super high but it still felt lighthearted and funny, and despite the entire plot hinging on two characters, it rarely felt slow or boring to me. Mesmerizing visuals as well. I have high standards for space sci-fi, and overall still really liked it.
  • Rating: 3.9/5

3. Princess Mononoke

  • Summary: A 1997 classic, but it did re-release this year and I saw it for the first time in theaters. I can't believe it's taken me so long to watch this epic. Absolutely stunning visuals, musical score, and a richly layered fantasy world that had me thinking about it for a few days after. Surprisingly mature and violent for a Studio Ghibli which I actually think made me like it even more. One of my favorite Anime movies for sure and I'm glad I saw it in a basically empty theater.
  • Rating: 4.5/5

2. Obsession

  • Summary: I can't remember the last time I felt this much adrenaline and shock in a movie. Bear was a completely selfish, insecure, and pathetic protagonist and his willingness to continue stripping Nikki of her free will in the face of increasingly horrific acts made for great cinema that I couldn't stop watching. I absolutely hated him. And it all built up to an insane climax that shattered my soul. Brilliant horror elevated by an incredible Inde Navarette performance. Not sure what it says about me that I need this type of experience just to feel something at the movies.
  • Rating: 4.5/5
